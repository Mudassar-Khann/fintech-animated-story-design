# Animation & Motion Psychology

### Spatial Orientation & Masking
- Use motion to explain spatial relationships (e.g., a modal sliding up from the bottom indicates it can be swiped down to dismiss).
- Use skeleton loaders or engaging micro-animations to mask system wait times and reduce perceived latency.
