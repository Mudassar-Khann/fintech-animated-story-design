# Pricing & Value Framing Psychology

### Status & Identity Signaling
- Elevating user identity and membership tier status.
- **Tier Badges:** "Founding Member", "Pro Tier", "Enterprise".
- **Highlight Badges on Pricing:** Highlighting the recommended plan with a distinct color pill ("Most Popular" / "Best Value").

### Component Placement
- Ensure the highest margin or most popular tier is visually elevated (scale, shadow, or distinct border).
