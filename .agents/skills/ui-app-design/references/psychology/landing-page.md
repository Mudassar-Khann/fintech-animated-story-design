# Landing Page Conversion Psychology

### Social Proof & Authority
- **Trust Bar:** Greyscale monochrome client logo array positioned immediately below the hero CTA.
- **Testimonial Cards:** Verbatim customer quote, reviewer photo avatar, verified buyer checkmark, star rating, customer title and company logo.

### Scarcity & Urgency (Verifiable)
- Highlighting authentic real-time resource limits to accelerate decision velocity.
- **Batch Cutoff Badges:** "Next deployment cohort starts Monday."

### Canonical Section Sequencing & Friction Rationale
* **Sequence:** Sticky Header \(\rightarrow\) Hero / Main Fold \(\rightarrow\) Social Proof (Trust Bar) \(\rightarrow\) Old Way vs. New Way (Comparison) \(\rightarrow\) Benefits & Product Features \(\rightarrow\) Pricing Tiers \(\rightarrow\) Objection Handling (FAQ) \(\rightarrow\) Final Action CTA \(\rightarrow\) Footer.
* **Cognitive Rationale (*The Why*):**
  * **Hero \(\rightarrow\) Trust Bar:** Immediately validates the bold headline claim with authority logos before skepticism sets in.
  * **Social Proof \(\rightarrow\) Old Way vs. New Way:** Front-loads trust so the user receptively evaluates the problem/solution contrast.
  * **Comparison \(\rightarrow\) Benefits & Features:** Establishes the pain point first so features read as relief/benefits rather than abstract specs.
  * **Benefits \(\rightarrow\) Pricing:** Establishes value perception *before* introducing financial cost, reducing price shock.
  * **Pricing \(\rightarrow\) Objection Handling (FAQ):** Addresses lingering purchase anxiety (cancellation, setup time, security) at the exact moment of commitment.
  * **FAQ \(\rightarrow\) Final CTA:** Provides a clean, frictionless final conversion point once all major objections are resolved.
