# Footer Component

* **Layout:** Multi-column link directory (4–5 columns) + bottom legal bar. Padding `64px` top, `32px` bottom.
* **Columns:** Brand logo & mission statement (Col 1), Product links (Col 2), Resources (Col 3), Company (Col 4), Legal & Social links (Col 5).
* **Bottom Bar:** Copyright text, privacy policy link, terms of service link, language selector dropdown.
